# Selectable product offers before production — 2026-11-07

## Problem and behavior

The previous Studio entry exposed long research sections before a seller could choose a product. An unobserved party content count still defaulted to ten. Making the field editable did not solve the choice problem.

Studio now opens with up to three selectable product offers in both simple and expert modes. Each card contains the actual product, content count, title, short description, proposed USD price, tag count and research sample size. Full description, thirteen tags and linked research sources open only on request. The nineteen digital categories are available in a separate picker. Manual topic/count entry and advanced research remain accessible deliberately.

Choosing a card accepts a six-hour server-side quote. Its exact title, description, tags, price, count and product identity enter the saved production workspace, external-assistant package and upload form. Client metadata overrides are ignored. A sequential retry returns the same accepted handoff. Merely previewing cards does not reserve a product name, record a prepared product or create an Etsy draft.

## Counts, evidence and repetition

When competitor content counts are unavailable, party design scopes follow the game structure: eighteen core activity pages, twelve player-facing escape-room pages, twenty-four distinct bingo cards or fifteen trivia-round pages. These are design experiments, not measured winning counts. Support, answer keys and covers are counted separately. Existing category-specific limits and manually requested valid counts remain enforced.

Candidates use category search samples followed by product-matched research. GET requests are memoized within one batch to avoid duplicate upstream searches; tests bound active-listing requests to six per batch. Fewer than three usable fresh offers are allowed. Failed research does not turn into invented fallback cards. The comparison covers a sample, not every competitor or verified bestseller sales.

Prepared and saved product concepts and recent suggestions inform bounded topic avoidance. Superficial style/color changes do not create a new concept. Browser QA exposed a remaining defect: a truncated competitor tag such as `christmas games prin` could become a separate suggestion. Automatic expansion and old discoveries now reject known catalog phrases cut inside a word. Complete whole-word topics remain eligible. This is not an unrestricted natural-language spelling verifier or a global concurrent uniqueness lock.

The listing preview is generated by the app's existing rules and metadata builders. Actual ChatGPT/Claude inference occurs only after the seller explicitly transfers the package. The app does not obtain API inference from a consumer subscription. Product files still require generation, download and file-level review; preview claims are instructions to fulfill and must match the final files before sale.
