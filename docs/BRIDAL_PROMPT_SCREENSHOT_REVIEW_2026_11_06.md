## Released changes

The fresh product-matched median and launch trial now enter the production brief from one canonical research record; stale machine seed summaries are replaced. Selected design terms are not presented as verified buyer preferences. Originality requirements no longer claim competitors lack a feature. Generic Wedding/Party/Groom words no longer become invented included-game names. Synthetic single-word party feature tags are not appended to shower-game tails.

Party-game counts distinguish core game pages from covers, host material, matching extras and keys. The production spec must enumerate actual game titles, questions/rules/host inputs, key requirements and a per-file page manifest before export. Regular party games use one core game per page; the conflicting multiple-cards-per-page instruction is removed. Formats must retain readable text and handwriting space, and cannot silently lose content. Escape-room rules remain separate.

C1-C10 are ten Etsy gallery assets. The actual content preview C4 is uploaded second; C2 follows it. C11/C12 are separately labeled Pinterest/social references. Buyer download files and marketing assets must remain separate. Traffic and cover-readability suggestions are tests, not proven performance facts.

The known accidental Winery generated-name collision is rejected for unrelated products. A phrase-level Turkish gloss explains bridal/wedding shower and bride-to-be. The AI disclosure instructions require description disclosure without assuming a universal checkbox. Blanket all-sales-final wording is replaced with digital-file support language that preserves applicable buyer rights. Actual content previews are distinguished from supposedly unedited composites.

Text-check success is labeled as text-rule checking. It does not certify final generated files, licensing, originality or future sales. The guard adds explicit conflicting-median, generic-game-name and unintended-name checks. Production contract revision 2 is required to restore a ready printable workspace; obsolete records remain stored but are not resumed as ready, and the app asks the seller to regenerate. Existing phone downloads are not deleted. Navigation-label widths are constrained to their tab; this still needs a new device screenshot to verify the intermittent cramped footer.

## Validation and limits

All 271 automated tests pass. Root/mobile TypeScript, browser-target Worker bundling and offline Android export pass. Regression cases cover the supplied package's contradictory medians, fake generic game names, count/support distinctions, ten gallery assets versus marketing channels, Turkish gloss, buyer-rights wording and obsolete-contract blocking. Existing actual-screen-to-Worker handoff/upload/draft checks still pass.

No live Etsy listing, Pinterest pin or customer message was published during QA. No seller files or stored products were remotely deleted or edited. The actual final game content, answers, layout and printing remain unverified because those files were not attached. A generated brief cannot establish legal compliance or sales success by itself.
